Alexander Pitchford and Maximilian Meyer-Mölleringhof representing QuTiP at the EuroSciPy 2026 in Krakow in July.
Max and Alex gave a tutorial which demonstrated qubit dynamics in closed and open systems and simulated the quantum teleportation protocol using the QuTiP-QIP package. 
They were supported by [Unitary Foundation](https://unitary.foundation/).
The conference was a great opportunity to learn about recent developments in key libraries.
It was also a chance to learn about contemporary approaches to Python development and discover new libraries and projects. 
There were also many interesting talks on applications, with a particular focus on astrophysics and cosmology. 
Of course, there were many useful contacts made and help found on specific current challenges. 
More detail can be found in this [UF blog post](https://unitary.foundation/posts/qutip_at_euroscipy2026/)
